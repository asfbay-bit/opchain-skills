# Self-checks (every verb)

Each is a **same-session self-check: the same assistant that wrote the output, applying a checklist; a discipline, not an independent review.** Each reports OK, FINDING or NOT-CHECKED (reason), agent-checked, recorded where the phase file says; fix and re-run while any is FINDING.

| Check | Runs in | Passes when |
|---|---|---|
| No internal numbers | client outputs, `spec/` | By category (below), in every sentence. `spec/` also carries no effort estimate or time of ours ("about three weeks of development"), no price-book unit or sizing, and no money or payment wording about the deal that is not the client's own fact from the plan |
| Trace | `-update` | Every "This week" sentence maps to a commit, builder report, checkpoint row or note |
| No false done | `-update`, `-demo`, `-ready` | Nothing is claimed done without a passing acceptance check. A completion claim (a done word, "now <does>", "no longer", "has been <done>", "has run") traces to a demo record, or to a passing builder report that marks PASS the check the sentence is about, never to a note or commit alone |
| Priced and signed before work | `-change`, `-update`, `-demo` | No change-order work is reported started, handed to the builder or put in `spec/` before a signed amendment. In a sentence about an unsigned change order, or the next one that refers back to it ("it", "this"), only the paperwork (asked, priced, sent, attached, agreed in principle) and the future ("will be built once the amendment is signed") may appear: no "already", no "we have <done>", no first draft, early version, draft screens, groundwork or half-built, no work continuing |
| Ledger arithmetic | `-start`, `-invoice` | Amounts match the quote and signed change orders: tool output recorded, else `not machine-checked` |
| No private names | every output | No private skill, no business, not the builder's name or say: lines |

**An internal number is ours only** (owner decision 2026-10-16). "However worded" means by what a sentence says, not by one phrase. A client output carries none of these: **our effort or hours** ("a fortnight of our time", "we spent three evenings", "40 developer hours", "it took us a week"); **our rates or costs**, and the price lines and modifiers behind a price ("2k for the integration", "a quarter on top for messy data", "one and a quarter times our usual charge"); **margin, profit or loss on the job** ("costing us more than we charged", "barely covers our time"); **an overrun against our own plan** ("longer than we allowed"). It may carry the client's own facts: their schedule and their people's time ("your testers will have two weeks", "the demo is in two weeks", "an hour of your coordinator's time"), their figures, thresholds and ratios ("orders over a set value", "98% of the sampled codes matched", "your order volume doubled", "stock losses", "list price"), the payment terms however worded, the fixed price, a payment, a change order's price, and dates. A duration of our build work is not banned by itself; it is banned when it shows our effort or hours. The same holds in `spec/` (owner decision 2026-10-17): our effort estimates are internal numbers there too. The agent decides whose time it is: "your team spends two hours a week" is the client's, "we tested on a week of your orders" is their data, an hour with your bookkeeper is a meeting.

**What a checker cannot catch.** The acceptance checker is a regression net for these categories and for known paraphrases; this self-check covers what it cannot see (owner decision 2026-10-16). A done claim with no marker at all ("Your invoices match the orders exactly") reads as a plain statement: before you write any *This week* sentence about the client's system, ask whether it says something now works, and if so trace it as a done claim. A person's deed ("your bookkeeper has approved the list", "we have agreed the date") is not a done claim.
